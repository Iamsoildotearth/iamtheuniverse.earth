Our creative project

I Am Soil Stories

Through a guided virtual journey, participants are invited to explore the incredible magical world of soil.

They can then respond through words, visual art, music, movement or other forms of creative expression. These individual responses can become part of a growing Global Gallery celebrating our interconnectedness with the Earth.

The experience combines creativity with accessible soil education, opening conversations about soil, food, nature and human wellbeing.

Good to know

Who can come?

Anyone from 5 to any age. Children, adults and mixed groups all work well.

Is it really free?

Yes. Everyone who runs the workshops is a volunteer.

Do I need any experience or knowledge?

No. No previous knowledge or experience is needed.

How long does it take?

About 1 hour, but we can offer more fascinating activities and experiments to extend the fun and learning.

Can schools and groups book?

Yes. Schools, community groups, farms and workplaces are all welcome, and it is free for everyone. Email workshops@iamtheuniverse.earth and we'll find a date that suits you.

What should I wear or bring?

Nothing special. If we are outdoors, dress for the weather.

Can I join online?

Yes. Many of our sessions run online.

Is the venue accessible?

We aim to use accessible venues, but can't always promise step-free access. Tell us what you need and we'll do our best.

How far will you travel?

Our facilitators can usually travel about an hour from their home area. Ask us who is nearest to you.

How it began

People only care about what they feel connected to

After many years of teaching about why we shouldn't cut down Amazonian rainforests, volunteering in marine conservation and working with regenerative growers, it's clear that we need something more effective than the methods that have been tried.

People can hear about how our food has lost much of the nutrients over the past 50 to 100 years and still shrug. They hear about how rainforests are still being cut down and that the plastic pollution in the oceans has now even been found in our blood. The facts reach the head, but not the heart.

I've seen how much more effectively learning takes place when we use experiential methods and a story, than a fact sheet.

So we built a methodology and a workshop that works the other way round. Feel first. Learn second. People step into the world of soil, water, air and light, make something of their own and then meet the science with their hands in real earth. Nobody has to be an expert.

It is a methodology built for behaviour change, because people look after what they feel a part of.

It's free. It runs in community venues and can come to you online. Everything people make can be shared in the Global Gallery.

Our vision

A different way of seeing soil

When people feel connected to the living systems that sustain them, new possibilities for understanding, creativity and care can emerge.

Our work brings together creative practice, community participation and soil education to help make soil visible, meaningful and relevant to people of all ages and backgrounds.

Research and partnerships

Taking soil stories into European research

We are developing a proposal for a Living Lab under the European Union's Horizon Europe programme, as part of the EU Mission 'A Soil Deal for Europe'. Living Labs bring farmers, scientists, communities and local authorities together to test practical ways of improving soil health.

Our proposal, called CULTIVATE, would add the human side. Trained volunteer facilitators would run I Am Soil Stories workshops alongside the farm-based research, helping communities understand and care for the soil their food depends on.
