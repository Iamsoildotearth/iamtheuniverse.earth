Reconnecting people with the living Earth

We explore how creativity, connection with nature and a deeper understanding of soil can help people rediscover their relationship with the Earth that sustains us.

Soil

Soil is more than something beneath our feet. It supports food production, biodiversity, water cycles and the ecosystems on which life depends.

Creativity

We use imagination and creative expression to communicate ideas and experiences that can be difficult to convey through information alone.

Connection

We create opportunities for people to explore their relationship with soil, nature and one another through shared experiences and storytelling.

Our creative project

I Am Soil Stories

Through a guided virtual journey, participants are invited to imagine experiencing the world from the perspective of soil.

They can then respond through words, visual art, music, movement or other forms of creative expression. These individual responses can become part of a growing Global Gallery celebrating our interconnectedness with the Earth.

The experience combines imagination with accessible soil education, opening conversations about soil, food, nature and human wellbeing.

Our vision

A different way of seeing soil

When people feel connected to the living systems that sustain them, new possibilities for understanding, creativity and care can emerge.

Our work brings together creative practice, community participation and soil education to help make soil visible, meaningful and relevant to people of all ages and backgrounds.
