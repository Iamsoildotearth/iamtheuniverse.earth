Meet the Soil Squad

Hello, soil explorers! Meet the tiny living things that look after the soil and help the food on your plate to grow.

Meet the squad

Soil looks still but it is full of life. Can you spot a mushroom, a blob, a water bear and a worm?

The squad

Who lives in the soil?

Fungi Fern

I am a fungus. My threads are called hyphae and they can link plant roots underground and carry water and food between them.

Proto Pete

I am a protozoan, a tiny one-celled creature. I gobble up bacteria and that helps to release food that plants can use.

Tara Tardigrade

I am a tardigrade or water bear. Some of us have survived being frozen and dried out and have even been into space.

The Backing Singers

We are bacteria. A teaspoon of healthy soil can hold billions of us and many of us help plants to get the food they need.

Be a soil explorer

Soil detective

Scoop a small handful of soil onto a plate. What can you see, smell and feel? How many different things can you find?

Jam-jar soil test

Half fill a jam jar with soil then add water and shake. Leave it to settle and watch the layers appear.

Creature hunt

Look under leaves and stones for tiny creatures. Count them gently then put them back where they live.

Watch a seed grow

Grow a bean in a clear jar with damp soil or paper towel and watch the roots appear.

Squeeze test

Squeeze a handful of damp soil. Does it crumble or stick together? Try soil from different places.

Draw the Soil Squad

Draw your own microbe band member and send it to us with a grown-up's help. It could join our Global Gallery.

Safety first. Ask a grown-up before you start and wash your hands after touching soil.
