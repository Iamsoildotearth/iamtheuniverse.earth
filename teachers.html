What happens

Explore, create, share

1. Explore

A guided 15-minute virtual journey into the incredible world of soil, meet the microbes and join in the fun.

2. Create

Learners respond in their own way through words, drawing, painting, music or movement.

3. Share

Share, reflect and then get hands-on with real soil. With permission, pupils' work can join our Global Gallery.

Citizen science

Soil testing in your school

Learners can do real science with soil, from simple tests to biodiversity counts in the school grounds. We are developing school soil-testing projects and Citizen Science Clubs and would love to hear from teachers who want to be part of them.
