EU Mission Collaboration

Integrating Citizen Engagement into Soil Research

We have developed a modular public engagement framework designed to complement proposals for regional Living Labs under the European Union's Horizon Europe programme, in alignment with the EU Mission 'A Soil Deal for Europe'.

Our framework, called CULTIVATE, is built to add the essential human and community dimension alongside farm-based research. By embedding trained volunteer facilitators to run I Am Soil Stories workshops alongside physical soil trials, we help consortia build the civic literacy and behavioural change necessary to support long-term soil health objectives.

We are keen to speak with research institutions, agricultural networks and lead coordinators who are developing a Living Lab proposal and want to add experiential public engagement and a citizen science component.

Our Framework

The Social Layer of Soil Science

Scientific data changes minds, but emotional connections shift habits. The EU Soil Mission estimates that 60 to 70% of soils in the European Union are unhealthy and the Joint Research Centre's soil health dashboard put the figure at 61% in 2023 (source).

To reverse this trend, technical findings must reach community culture. I Am Soil Stories acts as a translation layer, with a standardised workshop methodology, a live Global Gallery for co-designed citizen output and a network of educators.

Citizen science is part of the picture. Our work began with a call for a local citizen science soil-health monitoring pilot and engaging people in it remains central. Participants can add simple soil tests and biodiversity counts at green social prescribing sites and we are developing school soil-testing projects and Citizen Science Clubs.
